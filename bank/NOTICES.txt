JEE Practice — licenses and acknowledgments

Practice collection

JEE Main Question Bank by eQOURSE is distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. The publisher provides questions, answer keys, worked explanations, and diagrams. This edition reformats the publisher’s material into searchable records, assigns identifiers, flags missing fields, and preserves available source answers. It retains the 290 published images with unambiguous mappings used by this edition. The images retain their original bytes in the Base64-encoded JSON packs listed in licensed/figures/index.json. That index records each original logical path and its SHA-256. The collection also includes three adaptations with reworded or re-typeset questions and choices, and new solutions and narration. See notices/practice-sources.tsv for the credit and original source record of every adaptation and dataset entry.
